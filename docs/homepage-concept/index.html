From setup to first stone

A game-day workflow
built around curling.

Plan the season in your browser. Open Studio at the rink. Give family, friends and supporters one place to follow along.

01 / PLAN

Set up the game

Choose the event, opponent, date and local time. Add a game number if you want one. Reserve a YouTube watch link for a scheduled stream.

02 / CONNECT

Scan. Frame. Check.

Open Windows Studio and scan the camera invitations with your phones. Check both pictures, choose your audio and bring in the sponsors.

03 / BROADCAST

Share every end

Start the YouTube broadcast when ready. Score from Studio or invite a remote scorer. Finish with results and a replay link for your team page.

Windows computer

Runs Studio, receives the cameras and sends the finished broadcast. Exact supported specifications will accompany the installer.

One or two phones

Browser camera connections, secure mounts and charging power for the game. Two phones give you both camera views.

Reliable network

A local network that lets the phones reach the PC, plus reliable internet upload. Check the rink network before game day.

Optional wireless audio

A compatible USB microphone receiver and player microphones. Camera microphones can provide ambience. Use headphones to check the mix.

You also need a YouTube channel enabled for live streaming. A separate phone or tablet can handle remote scoring.

Before you start

A few good questions.

Can I manage the team away from the rink?

Yes. Use the website for scheduling and team information. The Windows computer runs Studio for the broadcast at the rink.

Do viewers need a CurlStreamer account?

Published team pages are public. The broadcast opens on YouTube and follows the visibility and viewing settings of that video.

Can someone else keep score?

Yes. Share the game's remote scorer invitation so another phone or tablet can update the scoreboard.

Does it automatically publish to Facebook and Instagram?

Direct social publishing is planned and depends on completing Meta setup and approvals. The current team page can display social profile links. The launch website should keep these capabilities distinct.

Can I change my team's public address later?

Choose carefully: the team's subdomain is fixed after first publication. You can update page content and control which sections are visible.

Where do I get Studio?

A public download page is planned with the current version, system requirements and installation steps. This concept does not distribute an installer.
