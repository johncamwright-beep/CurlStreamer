# Controlled YouTube rehearsal result

The September 2026 short rehearsal completed using the existing authorized team owner and connected YouTube channel. Exactly one unlisted broadcast and its owned stream were prepared. Auto-start/auto-stop stayed off; Go Live and End were manual.

## Verified

- About five minutes live, with approximately five and a half minutes of media.
- Native output counter exceeded 250 MB; independent YouTube reception and the application's Live lifecycle agreed.
- YouTube playback showed both full portrait cameras, sponsor, score and two verified direct camera paths.
- YouTube stopped receiving media after local Stop while the MKV continued growing.
- Manual End produced Stream Finished/Offline. Owned-resource retirement was confirmed and persisted on a fresh session verification.
- The recording finalized at 1,297.9 seconds and 974,470,742 bytes, with H.264 1920x1080 at 30 fps plus an AAC track. The final 15 seconds decoded successfully; the final picture retained both cameras and layout.
- The rehearsal sponsor was disabled and retained. Temporary activated processes stopped, and the replacement operator used the default-deny DLL with pairing/streaming disabled.

## Limits

The program was video-only; an audio bitrate suggestion is not audio acceptance. An initial YouTube preview error cleared on reload. The native stopped observation expired before it was captured: UI stop completion, independent No data and continued recording growth were verified, but a fresh native stopped reading is not claimed. Long endurance and rink acceptance remain deferred.
