Celebration media

lightsaber.mp4, cat-dance.mp4 and the source for trump-dance.mp4 were supplied
by the user for this CurlStreamer installation. The lightsaber foreground
PNGs are derived from the supplied lightsaber clip. Trump Dance preserves
the full portrait frame inside a square with generated animated side lights.
Original audio is retained.

These media assets are separate from the CurlStreamer software license.
This package does not assert a third-party redistribution license for them.
Personal headshots are stored separately and are not bundled in Studio.
